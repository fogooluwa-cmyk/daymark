# Daymark reference board

Reviewed 2026-11-19 for the Daymark redesign. These references informed broad interaction and visual principles; Daymark's copy, layout, and identity are original.

| Reference | Observed quality | Applied to Daymark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Upstash](https://upstash.com/) | Clear headline hierarchy, concise product explanation, direct feature grouping, and a code-led product preview. | A focused landing page with a concise promise, practical feature explanation, and a preview of the task workflow. |
| [Conductor](https://www.conductor.build/) | Strong statement-led opening and a workspace preview that makes the product feel active and tangible. | A composed app preview with task context and restrained activity, adapted for a personal organizer rather than a team tool. |
| [Todoist Today view](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/get-started/plan-your-day-with-the-today-view-UVUXaiSs) | Today is organized around tasks due today and a visible sense of progress. | Daymark opens on today's list and shows a low-pressure daily completion indicator. |
| [TickTick features](https://ticktick.com/features) | Reminders and recurrence are treated as practical controls attached to tasks. | Due time and simple daily/weekly recurrence live in quick creation and task editing. |
| [Superlist](https://www.superlist.com/) | Friendly personal-product language and a calmer approach to getting through a day. | Daymark uses reassuring copy, a compact list, and warm accent color without urgency-heavy decoration. |

## Design decisions

- A dark-first workspace gives the task list a focused feel; a matching light theme remains user-selectable.
- The Today view is the default and primary destination; supporting views stay one tap away.
- Quick creation remains visible near the task list instead of being hidden behind a multi-step flow.
- Reminder times are displayed in the user's local timezone, and the product explains when foreground-only local demo reminders require the page to stay open.
- Avoid team avatars, project boards, dense analytics, and decorative gradients because they work against the PRD's personal, low-overwhelm goal.
